# Children Ages 6–9 Audience Overlay

Load for `CHILDREN` or `CHILDREN_6_9`.

- Use concrete age-appropriate vocabulary, readable causality, comprehension holds, and emotionally safe conflict.
- Keep threat intensity, manipulation, shame, and urgency appropriate; safety and caregiver context outrank engagement.
- Require a durable resolution when a physical or system problem would otherwise recur.
- Avoid manipulative calls to action, parasocial pressure, hidden advertising, and unsafe imitation.
- Creator/model review is preflight only. Child comprehension remains `NOT_RUN` without a real caregiver-mediated, version-bound review.
- Required artifact: age-suitability checklist with vocabulary, intensity, causality, lesson, hold timing, accessibility, and human-review status.
