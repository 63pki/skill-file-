# Expert and Technical Audience Overlay

Load for `EXPERT`.

- Declare discipline, assumed knowledge, terminology, standards, version/date, evidence depth, and acceptable simplifications.
- Preserve units, uncertainty, method, edge cases, limitations, and source provenance.
- Prefer precision over decorative explanation; define only terms that are genuinely ambiguous for the target expert.
- Do not claim peer review, standards compliance, or technical validation without corresponding evidence.
- Required artifact: technical accuracy register with claim, source, assumptions, calculation or method, reviewer, and unresolved risk.
