# Regulated Claims Audience Overlay

Load for `REGULATED`.

- Register jurisdiction, product/service category, claim owner, evidence source, required qualification, disclosure, and approval status.
- Separate factual product description, measurable claim, testimonial, implication, comparison, and prohibited guarantee.
- Do not convert model output, prompts, metadata, or generated demonstrations into substantiation.
- Block release when required legal, medical, financial, safety, or policy review is missing.
- Required artifact: claim ledger linking each visible/spoken claim to evidence, qualification, placement, reviewer, and final status.
