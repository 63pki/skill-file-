# Accessibility Audience Overlay

Load for `ACCESSIBILITY`.

- Record relevant visual, auditory, motor, cognitive, language, and assistive-technology needs without assuming one audience is uniform.
- Plan captions, transcripts, audio description, contrast, text size, safe areas, reading speed, reduced motion, flash limits, and non-audio/non-color cues.
- Preserve meaning across accessible variants and record what was actually tested.
- Automated checks are preflight, not proof of lived accessibility.
- Required artifact: accessibility matrix with requirement, implementation owner, test method, status, limitation, and human-review evidence.
