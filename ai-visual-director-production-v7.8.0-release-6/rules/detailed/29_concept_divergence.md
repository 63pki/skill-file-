# 29 Concept Divergence and Originality

Before polishing a full project, generate genuinely different concept territories—not cosmetic variations.

## Three-direction minimum

1. **Evidence:** visible proof object, behavior, mechanism, or consequence.
2. **Tension:** contradiction, constraint, obstacle, reversal, or unresolved relationship.
3. **World:** a distinctive ritual, setting, design system, or visual rule belonging to the promise.

For each direction:

```text
Concept ID / one-line promise:
Audience tension:
One-frame visual thesis:
Story turn/payoff:
Memory device (image/object/phrase/gesture/color/sound):
Sound identity:
Visual language:
Category clichés rejected:
Production difficulty and control path:
Rights/claim risks:
Why it is original to this brief:
```

Automatically reject and replace a direction when it is interchangeable with another brand/story, is only a look rather than an idea, depends on category clichés or empty spectacle, lacks an honest payoff, has no memory device or sound identity, exceeds the available production-control path, or copies recognizable protected expression. Continue until at least two directions pass. Select by brief fit, distinctiveness, feasibility, rights, and payoff—not adjective count. Store rejected concepts as future tests, not failed prompts.
