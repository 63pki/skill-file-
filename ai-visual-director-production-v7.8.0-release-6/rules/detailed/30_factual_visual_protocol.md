# 30 Factual Visual and Reconstruction Protocol

For documentary, history, science, education, news-adjacent, product demonstration, or any real-world claim, distinguish evidence from visualization.

## Claim ledger

```text
Claim ID | exact claim | source | checked date | confidence/status |
spoken wording | visual treatment | disclosure | reviewer
```

## Visual status

Label accurately when relevant:

- authentic/owned footage;
- licensed archive;
- illustration;
- conceptual visualization;
- AI-generated reconstruction;
- dramatization;
- simulation or product demonstration.

Never present an AI reconstruction as authentic evidence. Do not imply real tragedy footage, a real person’s conduct, or a product result without reliable sourcing, authorization where required, and clear disclosure. Attribute unsettled facts and keep exact data/text editable in post.
