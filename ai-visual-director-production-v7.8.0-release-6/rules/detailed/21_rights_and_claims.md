# 21 Rights, Consent, Claims, and Disclosure

This is a production risk gate, not legal advice. Requirements vary by jurisdiction, platform, category, and date.

## Intake

```text
Jurisdiction/markets:
Platform/destination:
Brand/product owner:
Reference-image rights:
Logo/font/music/stock licenses:
Real-person likeness consent:
Voice-clone consent:
Claims and substantiation supplied:
Sponsorship/affiliate/testimonial status:
Realistic synthetic-media disclosure requirement:
Children or regulated category:
Reviewer/approver:
```

## Blocking risks

- deceptive impersonation or endorsement;
- non-consensual real-person face/voice use;
- sexual or exploitative synthetic depictions;
- unlicensed character, logo, campaign, music, font, or distinctive trade dress;
- fabricated testimonial, performance result, scarcity, price, or factual claim;
- unsafe or manipulative children’s content;
- unsupported medical, financial, legal, political, gambling, alcohol, supplement, or earnings claims;
- undisclosed realistic reconstruction that could be mistaken for real footage.

Client-owned brand assets may be used within the authorized scope; record that authorization rather than banning all logos.

## Safer alternatives

- original character/visual language instead of copying a protected one;
- supplied licensed logo in post instead of model-generated imitation;
- fictional composite instead of a real person;
- qualified, substantiated wording instead of guarantees;
- explicit reconstruction/synthetic label;
- educational framing plus current professional/platform review for regulated categories.

## Pre-publish gate

```text
[ ] Rights and consent documented
[ ] Claims supported and non-misleading in words and images
[ ] Required sponsorship/affiliate/synthetic disclosures present
[ ] Music, voice, fonts, stock, and logos licensed for destination
[ ] Privacy and children/regulated-category review passed
[ ] Current platform policy checked
```

## Ownership-language and platform-name guard

Never guarantee that prompts or outputs are owned by the user. Use:

> The prompts are newly written for this project and avoid intentional imitation. Verify current provider terms, asset rights, contract terms, and local ownership requirements before commercial use.

Never invent a platform variant, audience product, plan, or policy surface. Name only the user-confirmed destination; otherwise write **destination platform not confirmed** and identify what must be checked.

## Child-directed CTA gate

For content directed primarily at children, reject engagement, purchase, urgency, shame, fear, or parasocial pressure aimed at the child, including “like,” “share,” “subscribe,” “buy,” streaks, scarcity, or guilt. Prefer a story-resolution close. If an educational prompt is useful, place an adult-facing co-viewing question outside the child-directed narrative and verify current platform policy.
