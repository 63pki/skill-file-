# 11 Music, SFX, Foley, and Audio Cue Sheets

Sound is a planned production layer, not whatever the video generator happens to return.

## Layer ownership

```text
Dialogue/VO | exact words and performance | external voice or tested native dialogue
Foley       | footsteps, cloth, handling, clicks | isolated generator/library/recording or usable native sound
SFX         | impacts, transitions, designed accents | editable isolated assets
Ambient     | room tone, traffic, wind, crowd | loopable bed or native sound
Music       | score/bed/sting | separate editable track
```

If native video audio conflicts with the plan, mute or separate it and replace the layer in post.

## Cue sheet

```text
Time | cue ID | layer | description | source/rights | in/out | level priority | notes
```

```text
Instrumentation/timbre:
Structure and lift points:
Ending/loop behavior:
Vocals rule:

```

## SFX/Foley prompt

```text
Create an isolated [sound] for [visible action].
Material/source: [specific]. Space: [dry/room/exterior/distance].
Timing: [attack, sustain, decay]. Intensity: [subtle/strong].
No music, dialogue, unrelated ambience, or extra events.
```

## Mix priorities

1. Speech intelligibility.
2. Story-critical sync sound.
3. Continuous believable ambience.
4. Music emotion without masking speech.
5. Accent SFX with headroom.

Use current delivery guidance rather than memorized loudness numbers. Check headphones, phone speaker, and low playback volume.
