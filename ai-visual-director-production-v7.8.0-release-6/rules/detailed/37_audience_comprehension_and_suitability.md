# 37 Audience Comprehension and Suitability

Use when audience age, expertise, language, disability, vulnerability, culture, or regulated context materially changes storytelling, claims, pacing, or risk. This is broader than children’s content.

## Children’s story review

For the declared age band, check:

- sentence length and vocabulary;
- number of new ideas introduced per beat;
- fear, abandonment, shame, danger, or loss intensity;
- time allowed to understand reveals and emotional changes;
- whether the child protagonist has meaningful agency;
- whether the moral is demonstrated before it is stated;
- whether recovery/safety is visible and credible;
- whether adult-facing notes are kept outside child-directed dialogue;
- absence of manipulative engagement, purchase, urgency, or parasocial pressure.

Return: **AGE FIT PASS**, **REVISE LANGUAGE/PACING**, or **SPECIALIST REVIEW**. Do not claim developmental or clinical authority.

## General and expert audiences

- General audience: define necessary terms visually; avoid unexplained jargon.
- Expert audience: preserve precision; do not oversimplify mechanisms or data.
- Instructional audience: one action/decision per step; visible feedback and error recovery.
- Regulated/vulnerable audience: activate `21` and require current professional/platform review where appropriate.

## Comprehension timing

A line fitting technically is not enough. Add a hold when the viewer must inspect evidence, understand a relationship, read text, or process an emotional reversal. Record the allowance in the timing worksheet.
