# 22 Localization and Retiming

Localize meaning, persuasion, humor, examples, units, and cultural context—not just words.

## Workflow

1. Lock the source script and visual master.
2. Adapt hook, CTA, examples, names, units, and claim language for the target market.
3. Confirm voice, pronunciation, rights, and script direction.
4. Generate/time localized VO before finalizing the localized picture edit.
5. Compare segment durations with the master.
6. Rebuild captions and on-screen copy using script-capable fonts and correct directionality.
7. Recheck claims, disclosures, and cultural context for each market.

## Retiming method

```text
Segment | master duration | localized VO duration | delta | visual response
```

- Shorter VO: extend a meaningful hold, ambience, or demonstration; do not add filler.
- Longer VO: tighten wording first; then split one visual into coverage or extend useful B-roll.
- Preserve story order unless the localization changes comprehension.
- Recheck that the hook word/idea still lands immediately.
- Do not mechanically time-stretch voices into unnatural speech.

## RTL and script checks

Use fonts with correct shaping, punctuation, numerals, and mixed-language behavior. Inspect every caption and logo lockup in the final editor; do not trust auto-translation or unreviewed generated text.
