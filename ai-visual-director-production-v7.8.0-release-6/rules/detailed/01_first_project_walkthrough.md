# 01 First Project Walkthrough — 15-Second Product Ad

This tutorial demonstrates the system with a fictional brief. It is a workflow example, not evidence that media was generated or that results were measured.

## Brief

```text
Product: reusable insulated bottle, original unbranded design
Deliverable: 15-second vertical social ad
Audience: commuters who dislike lukewarm drinks
Action: visit product page
Rights: product images and brand assets supplied by client
Budget: lean test
```

## 1. Declare mode and concept

**Mode A — photoreal commercial.** North-star feeling: *quiet control during a chaotic commute.*

Three territories:

1. **Temperature contrast:** hot train, cold bottle condensation.
2. **Morning control:** messy desk, one perfectly cold drink.
3. **Time proof:** morning fill → afternoon condensation.

Choose Territory 1 because the benefit can be understood visually without an unsupported performance number.

## 2. Reference intake

```text
REF-PROD-01 → exact geometry and cap
REF-MAT-01  → brushed-metal finish only
REF-BRAND-01 → palette and logo for post; do not bake into generations
```

Check rights, remove conflicting angles, and select one front three-quarter product image as the identity anchor.

## 3. Timed board

| Shot | Time | Function | Frame | Action | Camera | Audio |
|---|---:|---|---|---|---|---|
| S01 | 0–3s | heat/problem | tight commuter handrail + warm hand | hand releases hot rail | controlled handheld | train rumble, cloth movement |
| S02 | 3–8s | sensory reveal | macro bottle in bag | bottle lifts; condensation catches light | slow push | bag zip, bottle lift |
| S03 | 8–12s | product use | medium product-in-hand | cap opens; visible cold vapor/condensation | locked | cap click, subtle room tone |
| S04 | 12–15s | payoff/CTA | clean hero frame | small light sweep only | locked | musical resolve; CTA in post |

## 4. Visual DNA

```text
Honest commuter realism; cool product against warm environment; 50–85mm lens family for product shots;
single motivated window light camera-left; brushed metal with controlled reflections and real condensation;
restrained neutral grade; no glossy plastic look.
```

## 5. Image anchor prompt — S02

```text
Create a vertical commercial keyframe of the exact insulated bottle from REF-PROD-01, half-revealed inside a commuter bag. Preserve the bottle silhouette, cap, proportions, and brushed-metal finish. Macro three-quarter framing; bottle on the lower-left third with movement space above. Warm train interior falls softly out of focus while the bottle stays cool-toned. One window key from camera-left creates controlled highlights and a clear contact shadow. Fine realistic condensation, no fake label, no text, no extra bottle, no warped cap. Leave the brand mark and CTA for post.
```

## 6. Video prompt — S02

```text
Use the approved S02 keyframe as the exact starting frame. Preserve its bottle geometry, material, bag, framing, and light direction. Camera: one slow physical push toward the bottle. Action: a hand lifts the bottle once from the bag; the condensation remains attached to the surface and catches the window light. Environmental motion: slight train vibration and soft fabric movement. Audio: bag fabric, bottle lift, low train ambience only. No dialogue, music, text, logo, shape change, or extra fingers. End on a clean macro of the cap and cold surface.
```

## 7. Voice and sound

VO, if used:

```text
Your commute runs hot. Your drink doesn’t have to.
```

Sound cue sheet:

```text
0.0 train bed starts | 3.0 bag zip | 4.0 bottle lift | 8.3 cap click |
12.0 music resolves | 12.2 CTA appears in post
```

## 8. platform-neutral editing surface plan

- Build 9:16 timeline using verified current delivery settings.
- Cut the cleanest fragments to the board timing; remove unstable clip heads/tails.
- Match product color and black levels across shots.
- Add supplied logo and CTA as editable overlays inside current safe zones.
- Keep dialogue/VO intelligible; duck music under it.
- Caption the VO and proofread manually.

## 9. Evidence-based review

Do not mark the project “approved” from the plan alone. Inspect attached outputs or ask the user to report:

- Did bottle shape/cap/finish match REF-PROD-01?
- Did condensation stay attached rather than float?
- Did the single camera move remain stable?
- Was the first three seconds understandable without sound?
- Did the CTA remain inside the platform UI-safe area?

Use `18` for the verdict and `17` for one-variable repair.
