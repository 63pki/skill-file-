## Failure example

Symptom: anchored bottle cap changes during lift.

```diff
KEEP: approved start frame, composition, lighting, slow push, duration
- Action: the hand grabs, twists, opens, and pours from the bottle
+ Action: the hand lifts the closed bottle once; the cap remains fully closed and unchanged
```

Reason: remove multi-step geometry stress. Acceptance: cap shape and closure state remain unchanged through the selected fragment. Owners: `17`, `18`.
