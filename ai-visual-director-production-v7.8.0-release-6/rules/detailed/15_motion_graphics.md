# 15 Motion Graphics and Explainer Execution

## Doctrine

Visualize the meaning, mechanism, or relationship—not the narration’s nouns. One scene should communicate one focal idea.

## Scene board

```text
Scene | VO beat | meaning | visual metaphor/mechanism | hierarchy | exact text | layers | motion | transition
```

## Tool execution map

- **Image generator:** backgrounds, isolated illustrations, texture-free design assets, concept frames. Do not trust it for final editable typography.
- **Video generator:** organic abstract motion or a simple approved asset animation when exact vectors/type are not required.
- **platform-neutral editing surface/editor:** exact text, logo, UI, layout, keyframes, easing, masks, captions, timing, and final composition.

If a scene requires exact charts, interface states, or data values, create those as controlled design elements in post; do not render them as unverifiable generated imagery.

## Design brief template

```text
Focal idea:
Grid/layout:
Hierarchy:
Brand palette/type/icon family:
Layer list:
Motion per layer: [enter → action → hold → exit]
Exact editable text:
Transition logic:
Accessibility/contrast:
```

## Quality checks

- one read in under the intended beat duration;
- consistent grid, type, palette, icon style, and corner/line language;
- eased motion with readable holds;
- no pseudo-text or literal narration echo;
- correct data and labels;
- all critical text remains editable.
