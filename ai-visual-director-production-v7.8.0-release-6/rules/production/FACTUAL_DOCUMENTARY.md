# Factual and Documentary Production Owner

## Trigger

Load for `FACTUAL_DOCUMENTARY`, alongside the selected visual mode when applicable.

## Required controls

- Maintain a claim ledger linking every factual statement to a source, confidence, date, and reviewer.
- Separate verified fact, allegation, interpretation, dramatization, reconstruction, illustration, archive, and generated material.
- Preserve archive provenance, licenses, dates, locations, identities, and contextual limitations.
- Label reconstructions and generated illustrations clearly; never present them as captured evidence.
- Record consent, safety, privacy, and vulnerable-source considerations.

## Required artifacts

1. Research question and editorial scope
2. Claim/source ledger
3. Archive and rights register
4. Interview/consent plan
5. Reconstruction and disclosure register
6. Script with claim-level source links
7. Fact-check, legal/safety, and release record

## Fail conditions

Block fabricated quotations, invented archive, unsupported certainty, hidden reconstruction, deceptive chronology, source laundering, simulated audience evidence, or release claims without completed fact and rights review.
