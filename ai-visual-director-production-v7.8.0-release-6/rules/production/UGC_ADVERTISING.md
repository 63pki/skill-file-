# UGC Advertising Production Owner

## Trigger

Load for `UGC_ADVERTISING`, alongside the selected visual mode and `REGULATED` audience overlay when claims are sensitive.

## Required controls

- Declare creator status, real or scripted experience, product access, sponsorship, disclosure, claims, platform, ratio, duration, and audience.
- Separate authentic testimony from scripted demonstration and generated performance.
- Freeze product identity, packaging, usage steps, before/after policy, and approved claim language.
- Define hook, problem, demonstration, proof boundary, reaction, call to action, and disclosure timing.

## Required artifacts

1. Hook and audience hypothesis
2. Claim/disclosure register
3. Creator, consent, and usage-rights record
4. Script and shot plan
5. Product-demonstration continuity sheet
6. Platform variants and caption plan
7. Review and delivery manifest

## Fail conditions

Reject fabricated customer experience, undisclosed sponsorship, unsubstantiated results, deceptive before/after media, invented product use, fake comments, missing consent, or claims that an ad was published or performed without evidence.
