# Architectural and Real-Estate Continuity Owner

## Trigger

Load for `ARCHITECTURAL_CONTINUITY`, alongside Mode A or Mode D when applicable.

## Required controls

- Freeze verified floor plan, room adjacency, openings, structure, materials, fixtures, orientation, exterior relation, and approved camera paths.
- Distinguish existing condition, proposed design, staging, approximation, and generated visualization.
- Never invent property features, views, dimensions, amenities, finishes, or code compliance.
- Track camera position, height, focal assumption, screen direction, daylight direction, and room-to-room continuity.

## Required artifacts

1. Verified property/floor-plan register
2. Geometry, material, and fixture locks
3. Camera-path and view map
4. Visualization and staging disclosures
5. Shot/frame acceptance list
6. Continuity comparison record
7. Claim, rights, and delivery checklist

## Fail conditions

Block impossible layouts, drifting doors/windows, material substitution, misleading scale, invented views or amenities, hidden virtual staging, unsupported dimensions, or delivery without disclosure review.
