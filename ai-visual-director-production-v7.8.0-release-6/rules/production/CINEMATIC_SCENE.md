# Cinematic Scene Production Owner

## Trigger

Load for `CINEMATIC_SCENE`, especially two-character dialogue and continuity-sensitive narrative scenes.

## Required controls

- Freeze characters, costumes, props, geography, entrances, exits, eyelines, axis, screen direction, emotional objective, and ending state.
- Map dialogue turns to measured timing, reaction beats, interruptions, silence, and edit handles.
- Define master, coverage, inserts, reverses, and any motivated axis crossing.
- Track hand/prop continuity, seated/standing state, gaze, performance intensity, and background continuity.

## Required artifacts

1. Scene objective and beat map
2. Character/performance locks
3. Geography and 180-degree axis plan
4. Dialogue/reaction timing sheet
5. Shot and coverage map
6. Continuity register
7. Motion/audio inspection and delivery record

## Fail conditions

Reject broken eyelines, unexplained screen-direction reversal, missing reactions, prop teleportation, dialogue without timing, identity drift, unsupported lip-sync claims, or scene-quality claims based only on isolated frames.
