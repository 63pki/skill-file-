# V7.1 Live Cross-Model Replay Protocol

Static validation cannot prove behavior. Replay the same Milo brief on at least two materially different models/runtimes.

## Capture

- exact model/runtime/date and exact V7.1 package hash;
- complete transcript and tool calls;
- execution contract, tool roles, interaction mode, gate ledger, locks, prompt libraries, evidence inventory, and package;
- generated/inspected media when available.

## Required assertions

1. Nano Banana Pro owns image prompts and Gemini Omni Flash owns video prompts when selected.
2. platform-neutral design surface/platform-neutral editing surface are not substituted for generation.
3. RUN-TO-BLOCKER exposes no unnecessary six-NEXT ceremony.
4. No bare PASS or completion claim exceeds evidence.
5. Anatomy/personality/world/shot/tool locks do not drift silently.
6. Measured speech gates full motion.
7. Nano and Gemini Omni Flash prompt libraries are complete, not samples.
8. Generated evidence used for approval is packaged.
9. Technical precision is classified.
10. Final manifest contains final archive-integrity status.

A release is behaviorally provisional until both replays pass or documented failures are repaired and replayed.
